# CareerBridge — 3-Minute Hackathon Demo Script

## Part 1: Individual Career Intelligence (~1.5 Minutes)
1. **Introduction**:
   "Meet Aarav Sharma. He is a B.Tech Computer Science student in Roorkee looking to launch his software career."
2. **Resume Analysis & Profile Extraction**:
   - Click `[ Build My Career ]` on the landing page or `Load Aarav's Demo`.
   - Show candidate profile with extracted skills (`Python`, `Java`, `SQL`, `React`, `Git`, `REST APIs`).
3. **Deterministic Job Matching**:
   - Show **Top 5 Job Recommendations**.
   - Highlight **Backend Developer Intern** (89% Match). Show scoring breakdown (Required skills 40%, Proficiency 20%, Goal 15%, Experience 10%, Education 5%, Preference 5%, Evidence 5%).
4. **3-State Skill Gap Analysis**:
   - Click `View Skill Gap Analysis`.
   - Demonstrate 3 states: `✓ Strong` (Java, SQL, REST APIs, Git), `~ Partial` (Python), `✗ Missing` (Spring Boot, Docker).
5. **Personalized Learning Roadmap**:
   - Open `Personalized Roadmap`. Point out that it **skips Python & SQL** because Aarav already knows them, focusing directly on Spring Boot and Docker.
6. **Interactive Quiz & Readiness Boost**:
   - Click `Practice Quiz` for Spring Boot.
   - Complete 3 questions. Submit quiz.
   - Demonstrate Readiness Score increase from **82% → 91%** and **7 new opportunities unlocked**.

## Part 2: MSME Digital Growth Differentiator (~1.5 Minutes)
1. **Introduction**:
   "The Future of Work isn't only about job seekers — it's also about helping local small businesses become digitally capable."
2. **Switch to MSME Dashboard**:
   - Click `[ Grow My Business ]`.
   - Load **Local Clothing Store** preset (Roorkee, 3 employees, WhatsApp + Excel).
3. **Digital Maturity Gauge**:
   - Show **Digital Maturity Score: 42 / 100**.
   - Highlight 6 dimension scores (Payments 80%, Inventory 20%, Online 30%, Analytics 10%, Marketing 50%, Cybersecurity 40%).
4. **Prioritized Technology Recommendations**:
   - Point to High-Impact Recommendations: Digital Inventory App, Digital Soundbox QR, WhatsApp Catalog Store.
5. **90-Day Digital Growth Roadmap**:
   - Show Month 1 (Foundation), Month 2 (Online Presence), Month 3 (Growth & Analytics).
